How NVIN Scanner handles your documents, device permissions, advertising, and privacy.

1. Introduction

NVIN Scanner (the “App”) is a document scanning and PDF utility application provided by NVIN Softwares Pvt Ltd (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

The App is designed to perform its core document and PDF operations on your device. Certain third-party services, particularly advertising services, may process information as described below.

2. Documents and information you use

NVIN Scanner may allow you to capture document images, import photos or files, crop and enhance images, generate PDFs, merge PDFs, view files, and access recently created documents.

  • Document content: Scanned images, imported documents, and generated PDFs are processed locally on your device for the App’s core functionality. We do not operate a document-upload or cloud-document-storage feature as part of these core operations.
  • Locally stored information: The App may store generated files, file names, file paths, and recent-file information on your device so you can find and use your documents.
  • Files you share: If you choose to share, print, or open a PDF using another app or service, that recipient or service may handle the file under its own privacy practices.
  • Advertising-related information: Third-party advertising technology may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, app interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to applicable permissions and consent requirements.
Important: “Offline-first” describes the App’s document-processing features. It does not mean that advertising services never connect to the internet or process data.

3. Device permissions

Depending on your device, Android version, and the features you use, the App may request access to:

  • Camera: To scan documents or capture images.
  • Photos, media, and files: To import images and PDFs and save or access documents you choose to work with.
  • Internet/network: To request and display advertisements and support any related consent functionality.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Android device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses or may use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads where implemented. AdMob and its partners may collect or process information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

Depending on your region and applicable law, the App or Google's consent tools may ask you to make choices about advertising-related data processing. Your choices may affect which ads are shown. The availability of particular ad formats and consent controls can vary by app version and location.

For details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy guidance.

We do not sell the content of your scanned documents to advertisers.

5. Local storage and retention

Documents and related recent-file records created or managed within NVIN Scanner may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Some exported or shared copies may remain in other device folders or apps and must be deleted separately.

Advertising providers may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements. We do not control their retention periods.

6. Security

Keeping document processing local helps limit unnecessary transmission of document content. However, no device, application, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We encourage you to keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive documents with untrusted apps or recipients.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Choose which documents to scan, import, save, or share.
  • Delete files and recent records available through the App or your device’s file manager.
  • Change App permissions in Android settings.
  • Manage advertising preferences through available in-app consent controls, if provided, and your device’s Google advertising settings.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests. Rights available to you may depend on your location and applicable law.

8. Children’s privacy

NVIN Scanner is intended as a general document utility and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s document content through a developer-operated cloud service. Advertising-related processing is subject to applicable platform policies and legal requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, third-party services, or applicable requirements. The effective or last-updated date at the top of this page will indicate when it was revised.
